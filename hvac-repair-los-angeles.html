Get Your HVAC System Working Properly Again

An HVAC system can develop problems for many different reasons. A system may stop cooling, produce weak airflow, fail to heat, make unusual noises, or cycle on and off more frequently than it should.

Professional diagnosis is important because the visible symptom does not always identify the actual cause. An HVAC technician can evaluate the system and determine what needs attention.

Affordable Heating and Air provides HVAC repair services for customers throughout Los Angeles County, with emergency service available 24/7.

Common HVAC Problems

Some of the most common reasons customers request HVAC service include cooling problems, heating problems, poor airflow, unusual system noises, and systems that will not start or maintain the desired temperature.

Repair or Replacement?

Not every HVAC problem means that the entire system needs to be replaced. Depending on the equipment, condition, age, and nature of the problem, repair may be an appropriate solution. A professional evaluation can help determine the next step.
