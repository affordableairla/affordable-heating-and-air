Signs Your HVAC System May Need Repair

Some HVAC problems are obvious, while others begin with smaller changes in system performance. Paying attention to unusual behavior can help you address problems before they become more disruptive.

Insufficient Cooling

Your air conditioner runs but does not provide the level of cooling you normally expect.

Insufficient Heat

Your heating system operates but rooms remain colder than they should.

Reduced Airflow

Air coming from the vents feels weaker than usual or some areas receive very little airflow.

Unusual Sounds

New rattling, grinding, buzzing, or other unusual noises may indicate that the system needs attention.

Frequent Cycling

The system repeatedly starts and stops instead of operating normally.

System Will Not Start

The HVAC equipment does not turn on when heating or cooling is requested.

A Practical Approach to HVAC Repair

HVAC repair is more than replacing a component. Identifying why the system is malfunctioning is an important part of determining the right repair.

Step 1

Listen to the Symptoms

Understanding what changed with the HVAC system can help provide useful information during the service visit.

Step 2

Inspect the System

The equipment can be evaluated to determine what components or operating conditions may be contributing to the issue.

Step 3

Explain the Options

Once the problem is understood, the appropriate repair or other available options can be explained.

Step 4

Restore Comfort

The goal is to restore dependable heating or cooling performance and help keep the system operating properly.
